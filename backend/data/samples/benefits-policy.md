# Employee Benefits Policy

Version 3.0 — Effective 1 January 2025
Policy owner: People Operations

## 1. Eligibility

All permanent employees working 20 hours or more per week are eligible for the
benefits described here from their first day of employment. Contractors and
agency workers are not eligible.

Benefit elections may be changed during the annual open enrolment window each
November, or within 30 days of a qualifying life event.

## 2. Qualifying Life Events

The following allow a mid-year change to elections: marriage or civil
partnership, divorce, birth or adoption of a child, death of a dependent, a
spouse's loss or gain of coverage, and a change between part-time and full-time
status. Supporting documentation must be provided within 30 days of the event.

## 3. Medical Cover

The company funds 100 percent of the employee premium and 75 percent of the
premium for dependents on the standard medical plan. A higher-cost enhanced
plan is available, with the employee paying the difference through payroll
deduction.

Cover begins on the first day of employment. There is no waiting period and no
exclusion for pre-existing conditions.

### 3.1 Dental and Vision

Dental and vision cover are included in both medical plans. Routine dental
examinations are covered twice per calendar year, and one eye test and one
frame allowance of 150 USD are covered per calendar year.

### 3.2 Dependents

Spouses, civil partners and children up to age 26 may be enrolled as
dependents. Dependent enrolment must be completed within 30 days of the start
date or of the qualifying life event.

## 4. Life and Income Protection

All employees receive life assurance at 4 times base salary at no cost. Income
protection pays 65 percent of base salary after a 13-week deferral period, for
as long as the employee remains unable to work, up to state pension age.

## 5. Retirement Savings

The company matches employee retirement contributions up to 6 percent of base
salary. Matching contributions vest immediately; there is no vesting schedule.

Employees are automatically enrolled at a 4 percent contribution rate and may
change or opt out at any time through the pension provider's portal.

## 6. Wellbeing

### 6.1 Employee Assistance Programme

All employees and their immediate household have access to confidential
counselling, legal advice and financial guidance, 24 hours a day, at no cost.
Use of the programme is never reported to the company in an identifiable form.

### 6.2 Mental Health Days

Employees may take up to 4 mental health days per calendar year. These are
recorded as paid sick leave but do not require a medical certificate and do not
count toward the certificate threshold in the Leave and Time Off Policy.

### 6.3 Fitness Allowance

The company reimburses up to 60 USD per month toward gym membership, fitness
classes or home exercise equipment. Claims are submitted through the finance
portal with a receipt.

## 7. Learning and Development

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 USD for courses,
conferences, books and certifications. The budget does not carry over between
calendar years.

Manager approval is required before booking. Where a certification costs more
than 3,000 USD, the company may ask the employee to repay a portion if they
leave within 12 months, on a sliding scale agreed in writing beforehand.

## 8. Family Support

The company contributes 300 USD per month toward registered childcare costs for
children under 5. Fertility and family-forming support is covered up to a
lifetime maximum of 20,000 USD per employee.

## 9. Other Benefits

- Company-funded annual health screening from age 40
- Cycle-to-work scheme with salary sacrifice
- 3 paid volunteering days per calendar year
- Home broadband contribution for permanent remote employees, per the Expense
  and Travel Policy

## 10. Leavers

Medical, dental and vision cover ends on the last day of the month in which
employment ends. Life assurance and income protection end on the final day of
employment. Retirement savings are the employee's to retain in full.
