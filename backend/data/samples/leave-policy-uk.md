# Leave and Time Off Policy — United Kingdom

Version 2.0 — Effective 1 April 2025
Policy owner: People Operations UK
Applies to: employees of the United Kingdom member firm

## 1. Annual Leave

Full-time employees in the United Kingdom accrue 27 days of paid annual leave
per calendar year, in addition to the 8 public holidays observed in England and
Wales.

Leave accrues at 2.25 days per completed month of service.

### 1.1 Carryover

A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried into the following
calendar year and must be taken by 31 March.

## 2. Statutory Sick Pay

Employees receive full pay for the first 20 working days of sickness absence in
any rolling 12-month period. Beyond that, Statutory Sick Pay applies at the
rate set by HMRC.

A fit note from a GP is required for any absence exceeding 7 calendar days.

## 3. Parental Leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to 26 weeks at full pay, followed by 13 weeks
at Statutory Maternity Pay. Secondary caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks at
full pay.

Shared Parental Leave is available in line with UK legislation and must be
notified at least 8 weeks before the intended start date.

## 4. Bank Holidays

Where an employee is required to work a bank holiday, they receive a day in
lieu plus a payment of 100 GBP.
