# Leave and Time Off Policy

Version 3.1 — Effective 1 January 2025
Policy owner: People Operations

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees on a permanent
contract. Contractors and agency workers are covered by the terms of their
engagement agreement and are excluded from the entitlements described here.

## 2. Annual Leave

Full-time employees accrue 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in
addition to public holidays observed in their country of employment. Leave
accrues at 2.08 days per completed month of service.

Part-time employees accrue annual leave on a pro-rata basis according to their
contracted weekly hours.

### 2.1 Carryover

A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried into the following
calendar year. Carried days must be taken by 31 March, after which they are
forfeited. Carryover beyond 5 days requires written approval from the
department director and is granted only where business demands prevented the
employee from taking leave.

### 2.2 Requesting Annual Leave

Requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 10 working days
before the intended start date for absences of 5 days or more, and at least 3
working days in advance for shorter absences. A manager must approve or decline
a request within 3 working days of submission.

Requests covering more than 15 consecutive calendar days require additional
approval from the department director.

## 3. Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick
leave does not accrue and unused days are not carried over or paid out.

A medical certificate is required for any absence exceeding 3 consecutive
working days. Absences of more than 10 consecutive working days move the
employee onto the long-term absence process described in Section 3.2.

### 3.1 Notification

Employees must notify their direct manager before 10:00 local time on the first
day of absence, by phone or direct message. Notification by email alone is not
sufficient.

### 3.2 Long-Term Absence

Absence beyond 10 consecutive working days is managed jointly by People
Operations and the employee's manager. Statutory sick pay applies from day 11
at the rate set by local legislation. The employee remains entitled to full
benefits coverage for the duration of the absence.

## 4. Parental Leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to 18 weeks of leave at full pay. Secondary
caregivers are entitled to 6 weeks of leave at full pay. Entitlement applies to
birth, adoption and surrogacy, and requires 12 months of continuous service at
the expected date of leave commencement.

Parental leave must begin within 12 months of the birth or placement date.
Employees must give at least 8 weeks written notice of their intended start
date.

### 4.1 Phased Return

Employees returning from parental leave may request a phased return of up to 8
weeks at reduced hours with no reduction in pay. Requests are approved by the
department director and must be submitted 4 weeks before the return date.

## 5. Unpaid Leave

Unpaid leave of up to 3 months may be granted at the discretion of the
department director, subject to business continuity. Benefits coverage
continues during approved unpaid leave; annual leave does not accrue.

## 6. Bereavement Leave

Employees are entitled to 5 days of paid bereavement leave for an immediate
family member, and 2 days for an extended family member. Additional time may be
taken as annual leave or approved unpaid leave.

## 7. Public Holidays

Employees observe the public holidays of their country of employment. Where an
employee is required to work a public holiday, they receive a day in lieu, to be
taken within 3 months of the worked holiday.
