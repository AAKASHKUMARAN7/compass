# Code of Conduct and Compliance Policy

Version 5.1 — Effective 1 April 2025
Policy owner: Legal and Compliance

## 1. Scope

This code applies to all employees, contractors, interns and board members, in
every location, and to conduct at company premises, client sites, off-site
events and online channels used for work.

## 2. Expected Behaviour

Treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Disagree with ideas, not
with people. Assume good intent, and raise concerns early and directly.

## 3. Harassment and Discrimination

The company prohibits discrimination and harassment on the basis of race,
colour, national origin, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation,
age, disability, pregnancy, marital status, or veteran status.

Harassment includes unwelcome conduct that creates an intimidating, hostile or
offensive working environment. It does not require intent to cause harm, and it
does not require the conduct to be repeated.

### 3.1 Reporting

Reports may be made to a manager, to People Operations, to Legal and
Compliance, or anonymously through the ethics hotline. Every report is
acknowledged within 2 working days and an investigation is opened within 5
working days.

### 3.2 Non-Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith, or who
participates in an investigation, is itself a serious violation of this code and
is treated as a separate disciplinary matter. This protection applies even where
the original concern is not upheld.

## 4. Conflicts of Interest

A conflict of interest arises where personal interests could improperly
influence a business decision. Conflicts must be declared in writing to Legal
and Compliance within 14 days of arising.

Situations requiring declaration include:

- Outside employment, consulting or board membership
- A financial interest in a competitor, supplier or customer
- A close personal or family relationship with a colleague where one party
  influences the other's employment, pay or performance rating
- Personal investment in a company the employee is evaluating for partnership

Declaring a conflict is not an admission of wrongdoing. Failing to declare one
is the violation.

### 4.1 Relationships at Work

Consensual relationships between colleagues are permitted, but must be declared
where a reporting line or evaluative relationship exists. People Operations will
arrange a reporting change so no one manages, rates or sets the pay of a partner.

## 5. Gifts and Hospitality

Gifts or hospitality with a value above 100 USD may not be accepted or offered
without prior written approval from Legal and Compliance. Cash and cash
equivalents, including gift cards, may never be accepted at any value.

Gifts to public officials are prohibited without exception, regardless of value
or local custom.

## 6. Anti-Bribery and Corruption

The company prohibits bribery in any form, including facilitation payments.
This applies to conduct by third parties acting on the company's behalf, and
agents and resellers must accept equivalent obligations in their contracts.

Anti-bribery training is mandatory annually for all employees in sales,
procurement and partnerships, and on joining for everyone else.

## 7. Insider Trading

Employees who become aware of material non-public information about the company
or any partner must not trade in the relevant securities, and must not pass that
information to anyone else. Designated employees are additionally subject to
closed trading periods announced by Legal and Compliance.

## 8. Fair Competition

Do not discuss pricing, market allocation, bidding intentions or hiring
restraint with competitors. If such a topic arises in any setting, stop the
conversation, leave, and report it to Legal and Compliance the same day.

## 9. Accurate Records

Business records must be complete and accurate. Never create a record intended
to mislead, and never destroy a record subject to a legal hold. Legal holds are
issued in writing by Legal and Compliance and override standard retention
schedules.

## 10. Speaking Externally

Only designated spokespeople may speak to press or analysts on the company's
behalf. Employees speaking publicly about their work in a personal capacity
should make clear they speak for themselves, and must not disclose Confidential
or Restricted information as defined in the Information Security Policy.

## 11. Investigations and Consequences

Investigations are confidential and conducted impartially. The person reported
is told the substance of the allegation and given an opportunity to respond.

Substantiated violations may result in coaching, a formal warning, removal from
a role, withdrawal of bonus, or termination of employment. Where conduct may be
criminal, the company will report it to the relevant authorities.

## 12. Annual Attestation

Every employee confirms in the HR portal each January that they have read this
code and have declared any conflicts of interest. Attestation is mandatory and
non-completion is escalated to the department director.
