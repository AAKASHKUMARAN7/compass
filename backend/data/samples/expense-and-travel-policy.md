# Expense and Travel Policy

Version 2.4 — Effective 1 March 2025
Policy owner: Finance

## 1. Principles

Employees are expected to spend company money as carefully as they would spend
their own. Every expense must be necessary, reasonable and supported by an
itemised receipt. Where this policy is silent, employees should ask Finance
before incurring the cost rather than seek forgiveness afterwards.

## 2. Submission and Approval

Expenses must be submitted through the finance portal within 30 days of being
incurred. Claims submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed except in
documented exceptional circumstances approved by the Finance Director.

Approval thresholds are as follows:

- Up to 250 USD: direct manager approval
- 251 to 2,000 USD: department director approval
- Above 2,000 USD: Finance Director approval, required in advance

Reimbursement is paid with the next scheduled payroll run following approval.

## 3. Travel Booking

All flights, rail travel and accommodation must be booked through the corporate
travel platform. Bookings made outside the platform are reimbursed only where
the platform was unavailable and Finance approved the alternative in advance.

### 3.1 Air Travel

Economy class is the standard for all flights under 6 hours of scheduled flight
time. Premium economy is permitted for flights of 6 hours or more. Business
class requires Finance Director approval in advance and is normally reserved for
flights exceeding 10 hours.

Flights should be booked at least 14 days in advance where the trip is known.

### 3.2 Accommodation

Nightly accommodation limits, excluding tax, are:

- Tier 1 cities (New York, London, Singapore, Tokyo, San Francisco): 320 USD
- Tier 2 cities: 220 USD
- All other locations: 160 USD

Costs above these limits require department director approval and a written
justification attached to the claim.

### 3.3 Ground Transport

Standard-class rail and economy ride-hailing are reimbursed in full. Rental
cars require prior manager approval. Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at the
statutory mileage rate for the country of travel.

## 4. Meals and Subsistence

Daily meal allowances while travelling on company business are:

- Breakfast: 20 USD
- Lunch: 30 USD
- Dinner: 55 USD

Alcohol is not reimbursable except at a client entertainment event with prior
manager approval. Meals provided by the airline, hotel or conference may not be
claimed separately.

## 5. Client Entertainment

Client entertainment requires prior approval from a department director. The
claim must record the names and organisations of all attendees. The per-head
limit is 120 USD, inclusive of tax and service.

## 6. Home Office and Equipment

New employees receive a one-time home office allowance of 750 USD, claimable
within the first 90 days of employment. The allowance covers desk, chair,
monitor and peripherals. Equipment purchased under this allowance remains
company property and must be returned on termination of employment.

Monthly internet costs are reimbursed up to 50 USD for employees on a permanent
remote contract.

## 7. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are not reimbursed under any circumstances: traffic and parking
fines, personal travel extensions, in-flight entertainment and duty-free
purchases, hotel minibar items, spa and gym charges, and travel insurance
purchased separately from the corporate policy.
