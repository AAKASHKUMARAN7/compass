# Leave and Time Off Policy — India

Version 2.0 — Effective 1 April 2025
Policy owner: People Operations India
Applies to: employees of the India member firm

## 1. Earned Leave

Earned leave is also referred to as annual leave or privilege leave. The India
entitlement replaces the global annual leave entitlement for employees of the
India member firm.

Full-time employees in India accrue 22 days of earned leave per calendar year,
in addition to 10 gazetted public holidays and 2 optional restricted holidays.

Earned leave accrues at 1.83 days per completed month of service.

### 1.1 Carryover and Encashment

Up to 30 earned leave days may be carried forward. Balances above 30 days are
encashed at basic salary each March.

## 2. Casual and Sick Leave

Employees receive 7 days of casual leave and 12 days of sick leave per calendar
year. Casual leave cannot be carried forward and lapses at year end.

A medical certificate is required for sick absence exceeding 3 consecutive days.

## 3. Maternity and Paternity Leave

Maternity leave is 26 weeks at full pay for the first two children, and 12
weeks thereafter, in line with the Maternity Benefit Act. Paternity leave is 10
working days at full pay.

Creche facilities or a creche allowance are provided in line with statutory
requirements.

## 4. Public Holidays

The India holiday calendar is published each December. Employees may select 2
restricted holidays from the published list.
