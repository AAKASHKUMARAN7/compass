# Compensation Policy

Version 2.2 — Effective 1 January 2025
Policy owner: People Operations

## 1. Principles

Compensation is set against the market for the role, the location and the level,
and is reviewed on a fixed annual cycle rather than by individual negotiation.
The company targets the 60th percentile of the relevant market for base salary.

## 2. Pay Structure

Every role is mapped to a level from L1 to L8, and each level has a published
salary band with a minimum, midpoint and maximum for each pay location. Bands
are reviewed annually against external market data.

Employees can view their level, band and position within the band in the HR
portal at any time.

### 2.1 Position in Band

A new joiner is normally placed between the minimum and the midpoint of the
band. Placement above the midpoint requires approval from the department
director and People Operations, and must be justified by prior experience or a
competing offer.

## 3. Annual Review Cycle

The compensation review runs once per year, effective 1 April. Proposals are
submitted by managers in February and approved by the compensation committee in
March.

Employees who join after 1 October are not eligible for the following April
review and are next reviewed in the subsequent cycle.

### 3.1 Merit Increases

Merit increases are funded from a company-wide budget expressed as a percentage
of payroll. Allocation is at manager discretion within that budget, guided by
performance rating and position in band.

An employee below the band minimum is brought to the minimum at the next review
regardless of performance rating.

## 4. Promotions

Promotions are approved twice per year, effective 1 April and 1 October. A
promotion moves the employee to the new level's band and carries a minimum
increase of 8 percent, or to the new band minimum, whichever is greater.

Promotion cases are written by the manager and reviewed by a cross-functional
panel. An employee must have been in their current level for at least 12 months
to be eligible, except where the panel agrees the role has changed materially.

## 5. Bonus

The annual bonus is discretionary and depends on both company performance
against plan and individual performance rating. Target bonus percentages by
level are:

- L1 to L3: 5 percent of base salary
- L4 to L5: 10 percent of base salary
- L6 to L7: 15 percent of base salary
- L8: 20 percent of base salary

Bonus is paid in May for the preceding calendar year. Employees must be
employed and not under notice on the payment date to receive it. Employees who
joined during the bonus year receive a pro-rata amount based on completed
months of service.

## 6. Overtime and On-Call

Employees in roles classified as non-exempt are paid overtime at 1.5 times the
hourly rate for hours worked beyond 40 in a week, and at 2 times the hourly rate
for hours worked on a public holiday.

Engineers on the production on-call rota receive an on-call allowance of 200 USD
per week of primary rota coverage, plus time in lieu for any incident worked
outside contracted hours.

## 7. Referral Bonus

An employee who refers a successful candidate receives a referral bonus of
2,000 USD for a standard role and 4,000 USD for a role designated
business-critical. The bonus is paid after the referred employee completes 3
months of service. Hiring managers and recruiters are not eligible for referral
bonuses on roles they own.

## 8. Payroll

Salaries are paid monthly on the last working day of the month. Payslips are
available in the HR portal from two working days before the payment date.

Payroll corrections raised before the 15th of the month are settled in that
month's run; corrections raised after the 15th are settled in the following
month, or by off-cycle payment where the error exceeds 500 USD.

## 9. Confidentiality

Salary bands and level structures are Internal information and may be discussed
freely within the company. Individual compensation details of other employees
are Confidential and must not be disclosed.
