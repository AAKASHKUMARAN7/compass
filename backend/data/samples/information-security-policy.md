# Information Security Policy

Version 4.0 — Effective 1 February 2025
Policy owner: Information Security

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the minimum security controls every employee must follow to
protect company and customer data. It applies to all employees, contractors and
third parties with access to company systems, on any device and in any location.

## 2. Account Security

### 2.1 Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters and unique to each system. Reuse of a
password across a company system and a personal service is prohibited. All
employees must store credentials in the company-provided password manager.
Passwords are not rotated on a schedule; they are changed immediately on any
suspicion of compromise.

### 2.2 Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on every company system that supports
it. Hardware security keys are required for anyone with administrative access to
production systems. SMS-based verification is not accepted as a second factor.

### 2.3 Access Reviews

System owners review access lists quarterly. Access is granted on a
least-privilege basis and revoked within 24 hours of a role change or
departure.

## 3. Device Security

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled, the managed endpoint
agent installed, and automatic screen lock set to 5 minutes or less. Operating
system and browser updates must be applied within 14 days of release.

Personal devices may be used to access email and chat only, and only when
enrolled in mobile device management. Personal devices must not be used to
store customer data or source code.

### 3.1 Lost or Stolen Devices

A lost or stolen device must be reported to the Security team within 2 hours of
discovery, at any hour, through the security hotline or the incident channel.
Do not wait until the next working day.

## 4. Data Handling

Data is classified in four tiers: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted.

- **Public**: approved for external release without restriction
- **Internal**: default for company material; not for external sharing
- **Confidential**: customer data, financial records, employee records
- **Restricted**: credentials, encryption keys, unreleased financial results

Confidential and Restricted data may only be stored in approved company
systems. Copying such data to personal cloud storage, personal email or an
unencrypted removable drive is prohibited and is treated as a security
incident.

### 4.1 Customer Data

Customer data may only be accessed for a documented business reason. Production
customer data must never be copied into a development or test environment.
Requests for a production data sample go to the Data Protection Officer.

### 4.2 Generative AI Tools

Confidential and Restricted data must not be entered into any public generative
AI tool. Approved enterprise AI tools with a signed data processing agreement
may be used with Internal data. Source code may only be shared with AI tooling
approved by Information Security.

## 5. Incident Reporting

Any suspected security incident must be reported within 2 hours of discovery
through the incident channel or the security hotline. This includes suspected
phishing, accidental disclosure, malware alerts and lost devices.

Employees who report an incident in good faith are never penalised, including
where the employee caused the incident. Delayed reporting, not the incident
itself, is what turns a contained problem into a breach.

## 6. Phishing and Social Engineering

Do not act on any request for credentials, payment or data that arrives by
unexpected email, message or phone call, even where it appears to come from a
company leader. Verify the request through a known internal channel before
acting. Report the attempt through the incident channel.

## 7. Physical Security

Visitors must be signed in and escorted at all times in office space.
Workstations must be locked when unattended. Printed Confidential material must
be collected immediately and disposed of in secure shredding bins.

## 8. Non-Compliance

Breach of this policy may result in withdrawal of system access and
disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment. Where a
breach involves customer data, regulatory notification obligations may apply and
are managed by the Data Protection Officer.
