# Workplace and Remote Work Policy

Version 2.6 — Effective 1 May 2025
Policy owner: Workplace Operations

## 1. Working Model

The company operates a hybrid model. Employees assigned to an office location
attend the office at least 2 days per week, with the specific days agreed within
the team so collaboration is concentrated rather than scattered.

Some roles are designated fully remote at the point of hire. Those employees
have no attendance requirement and are covered by Section 5.

## 2. Working Hours

### 2.1 Core Hours

Employees must be available and reachable between 10:00 and 16:00 in their
assigned working timezone. Outside core hours, employees manage their own
schedule.

Standard contracted hours are 40 per week. Meetings should not be scheduled
outside core hours without agreement from every attendee.

### 2.2 Flexible Start and Finish

Start and finish times may be varied by up to 2 hours without approval, provided
core hours are covered and the change is visible in the employee's calendar.

### 2.3 Compressed Weeks

An employee may request a compressed schedule of 4 longer days per week. Requests
go to the direct manager and are reviewed against team coverage. Approved
arrangements are reviewed every 6 months.

## 3. Requesting a Change to Working Pattern

Requests to change working location, hours or pattern are submitted through the
HR portal. A manager must respond within 15 working days. A refusal must state
the business reason in writing, and the employee may ask People Operations to
review it.

## 4. Office Facilities

### 4.1 Desks and Booking

Offices operate on a hot-desking basis. Desks are booked through the workplace
app up to 14 days in advance. A booking not claimed by 11:00 is released
automatically.

Employees with an accessibility requirement may be assigned a permanent desk;
requests go to Workplace Operations and do not require disclosure of a diagnosis
to the manager.

### 4.2 Meeting Rooms

Rooms are booked through the calendar system. Rooms held but unused for 15
minutes are released. Rooms seating 8 or more may not be block-booked for
recurring meetings of fewer than 4 attendees.

### 4.3 Visitors

All visitors sign in at reception and are escorted at all times. The hosting
employee is responsible for their visitor, including evacuation in an emergency.

### 4.4 Building Access

Access badges are personal and must not be shared or lent. Do not hold doors open
for anyone without a visible badge, including people you recognise. Lost badges
must be reported to Workplace Operations the same day.

## 5. Remote Work

### 5.1 Workspace Requirements

Remote employees are responsible for a workspace that is safe, private enough
for confidential calls, and has a reliable internet connection of at least 25
Mbps download.

### 5.2 Equipment

The company provides a laptop, monitor, keyboard and mouse to every employee
regardless of location. The one-time home office allowance and monthly internet
contribution are set out in the Expense and Travel Policy.

Equipment remains company property and must be returned within 10 working days
of the final day of employment, using the prepaid return label provided.

### 5.3 Working from Another Country

Working from a country other than the contracted country of employment is
permitted for up to 20 working days per calendar year, and requires approval
from People Operations in advance because of tax and immigration exposure.
Requests should be submitted at least 15 working days before travel.

Longer arrangements require a formal relocation and are considered case by case.

## 6. Health and Safety

Employees must complete the workstation self-assessment on joining and again
each January. Workplace Operations arranges an assessment and any adjustments
where the self-assessment indicates a need, for office and home workspaces
alike.

Accidents, near misses and unsafe conditions must be reported through the
workplace app on the day they occur.

### 6.1 First Aid and Fire

Each office displays the names of trained first aiders and fire marshals at
reception and on every floor. Fire drills are held twice a year and
participation is mandatory.

## 7. Office Closures and Severe Weather

Where an office closes at short notice, employees work from home for the
duration. Where working from home is not possible, the time is treated as paid
and is not deducted from annual leave.

## 8. Workplace Conduct

The office is a shared space. Take calls in booths or meeting rooms rather than
at desks, clear desks and bookable spaces at the end of the day, and follow the
kitchen and shared-area guidance posted on each floor.

Smoking and vaping are permitted only in designated external areas. Alcohol is
not permitted in the office outside company-sanctioned events.

## 9. Pets and Children

Assistance animals are welcome in all offices. Other pets are permitted only in
offices designated pet-friendly, and only where the pet is registered in advance
with Workplace Operations.

Children may visit the office briefly but the office is not a substitute for
childcare arrangements.
