# Sabbatical Leave Policy

Version 1.0 — Effective 1 October 2025
Policy owner: People Operations

## 1. Eligibility

Employees become eligible for a sabbatical after 5 years of continuous
service. Eligibility is renewed every 5 years thereafter, so an employee may
take a second sabbatical after 10 years of service.

Part-time employees are eligible on the same service basis. Contractors and
agency workers are not eligible.

## 2. Duration and Pay

An eligible employee may take up to 6 consecutive weeks of sabbatical leave at
full base salary. Bonus and commission are not paid for the sabbatical period.

Sabbatical leave is in addition to annual leave and does not reduce the annual
leave entitlement described in the Leave and Time Off Policy.

## 3. Requesting a Sabbatical

Requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 3 months before the
intended start date. Approval is granted by the department director, who must
respond within 15 working days.

A request may only be declined where the absence would create a critical
coverage gap. A decline must state the business reason in writing and offer an
alternative start date within the following 6 months.

## 4. During the Sabbatical

Benefits coverage, including medical and life assurance, continues unchanged.
Annual leave continues to accrue. Retirement contributions continue at the
normal rate.

Employees are not expected to be reachable and must not be contacted for
routine work matters.

## 5. Return to Work

The employee returns to the same role, level and salary band. A phased return
of up to 2 weeks at reduced hours may be requested, with no reduction in pay.

An employee who resigns within 6 months of returning must repay 50 percent of
the salary paid during the sabbatical.
