# Company Vehicle Policy

Version 1.0 — Effective 1 October 2025
Policy owner: Finance

## 1. Eligibility

A company vehicle is provided to employees at level L6 and above, and to any
employee whose role requires driving more than 15,000 km per year on company
business.

Employees not eligible for a vehicle may claim mileage for personal vehicle use
under the Expense and Travel Policy.

## 2. Requesting a Vehicle

Requests are submitted through the finance portal and require approval from the
department director and the Finance Director. Approved requests are fulfilled
within 8 weeks.

The employee must hold a valid driving licence with no more than 6 penalty
points, and must provide a copy annually.

## 3. Vehicle Categories

Monthly lease budgets by level, inclusive of insurance and maintenance:

- L6: 600 USD per month
- L7: 750 USD per month
- L8 and above: 950 USD per month

An employee may select a vehicle above their budget and pay the difference
through payroll deduction.

## 4. Personal Use

Personal use is permitted for the employee and their immediate family. Any
resulting taxable benefit is reported to the relevant tax authority and is the
employee's liability.

The vehicle must not be used for any secondary employment or for commercial
hire.

## 5. Fuel and Charging

Fuel and charging costs incurred on company business are reimbursed in full
against receipts. Fuel for personal mileage is not reimbursed.

Electric vehicles are the default. A home charger is installed at company cost
where the employee's residence permits it.

## 6. Accidents and Return

Accidents must be reported to Finance and the insurer within 24 hours,
regardless of fault. The insurance excess of 500 USD is payable by the employee
where the employee is at fault.

The vehicle must be returned within 5 working days of the final day of
employment.
